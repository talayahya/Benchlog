## Narration and example speech

Presentation narration: stock Microsoft **en-GB-SoniaNeural**, generated through edge-tts. This is a disclosed synthetic narrator, not Tala's voice or a voice clone. The script is in [seminar-narration.md](seminar-narration.md), and source clips and word timings are in [narration](narration/).

The three synthetic example utterances are Windows text-to-speech fixtures from `public/examples/`. The application really transcribed these files through local Whisper during the documented checks. Presentation narration was generated separately and is not evidence about application speech quality.
